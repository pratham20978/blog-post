# LinkedIn image prompts — what-the-nvidia-stack-actually-is

Six alternative image prompts, one per shared feed card, for the abstract editorial-illustration
route. These are not descriptions of the rendered cards in `assets/social/`; those are the
shipped assets and they carry their own text. These prompts produce wordless illustrations in a
single consistent visual world, for use when a carousel needs imagery rather than typography.

The recurring objects across all six frames: thin stacked plates, a shallow plinth, a solid base
block fused to that plinth, fine alignment rods, and shallow carrier trays. Keeping those objects
constant is what makes the six read as one sequence.

## 01 — One block, five plates

- Narrative beat: One thing people call CUDA is really five separate plates with different makers.
- File: `linkedin-01-one-block-five-plates.png`
- Alt text: A single solid block separates into five thin plates of different materials, the lowest two cut from one piece.

### Prompt

```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE
A 4:5 LinkedIn carousel opening frame that shows an apparently single object turning out to be five separate parts.

SUBJECT
A monolithic rectangular block stands on a shallow white-smoke plinth. It has separated along four horizontal seams into five thin plates, each drifting a few millimetres apart so the gaps are unmistakable. The plates are the same footprint but visibly different thicknesses and surface treatments.

KEY DETAILS
Establish the recurring objects for the whole sequence: thin stacked plates, a shallow plinth, and a solid base block fused to it. The lowest two plates are cut from the same darker material as the plinth and remain joined to each other; the upper three are lighter and clearly separable. Place one small atomic-tangerine chamfer along the seam where the joined pair meets the separable three. Arrange the stack slightly left of centre in a three-quarter orthographic view with generous space to the right.

CONSTRAINTS
4:5 portrait frame, designed for a LinkedIn carousel. Editorial technical
illustration with flat vector-like shapes, fine consistent line work, subtle
paper grain, and generous negative space. Palette strictly limited to
jet-black #2d3142, blue-slate #4f5d75, silver #bfc0c0, white-smoke #f5f5f5,
and atomic-tangerine #eb6c36. White-smoke dominates; atomic-tangerine appears
once on the focal element and covers less than five percent of the frame.
Maintain the same visual world, scale language, line weight, palette, and
recurring objects across every frame in the sequence. One idea per frame.
No text, letters, numbers, labels, captions, logos, watermarks, signatures, UI
chrome, photorealism, stock-photo people, glossy 3D, lens flare, or drop
shadows. Keep meaningful content inside a five percent safe margin and use
large shapes that remain legible at mobile-feed size.
```

- File: `linkedin-02-base-cannot-be-delivered.png`
- Alt text: Carrier trays deliver thin plates onto a stack, while the solid base block stays fused to the plinth beneath.

### Prompt

```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE
A 4:5 LinkedIn carousel frame contrasting parts that can be delivered with one that cannot.

SUBJECT
Three shallow carrier trays float in from the upper right, each holding one thin plate, descending toward a partially built stack. Beneath the stack, a solid base block is fused into the plinth with no seam at all, clearly of one piece with the ground.

KEY DETAILS
Reuse the plates, trays and plinth established in the first frame. Draw the three incoming trays at staggered heights along a shallow diagonal so the delivery reads as motion without arrows. Show a visible seam between every delivered plate and the one below it, and no seam whatsoever between the base block and the plinth. Put the single atomic-tangerine accent on the fused joint at the base. Leave the upper-left third as open white-smoke.
```

```text
SUBJECT
```

## 04 — Nine counters on one case

- File: `linkedin-04-nine-counters-on-one-case.png`
- Alt text: A sealed case carries one large counter dial while nine smaller dials inside it rest at visibly different positions.

### Prompt

```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE
A 4:5 LinkedIn carousel frame about a single labelled whole containing parts that disagree.

SUBJECT
A shallow rectangular case lies open, face up. Its lid carries one large circular counter dial. Inside, nine smaller circular dials are set in a three-by-three grid, each needle resting at a clearly different angle from the lid's dial and from one another.

KEY DETAILS
Render the dials as flat concentric circles with a single fine needle each, no numerals or tick labels of any kind. Make the lid dial noticeably larger so the hierarchy is obvious. Set two of the nine inner needles at the extremes of their travel, one near the start and one near the end, and give the extreme pair the frame's only atomic-tangerine needles. Keep the case in the recurring material language of the plates. Centre the case with generous white-smoke around it.
```

### Prompt

```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE
A 4:5 LinkedIn carousel frame about an unrequested part arriving with a requested one.

SUBJECT
A carrier tray descends holding one modest crate. Strapped to the underside of that crate, and considerably larger and heavier than it, is a second crate that visibly dominates the load. The tray tilts slightly under the imbalance.

KEY DETAILS
Reuse the carrier tray from the second frame. Make the lower crate at least twice the volume of the upper one so the size inversion is immediately readable. Draw the two binding straps clearly so the attachment is understood as a physical consequence, not a choice. Give the large lower crate the single atomic-tangerine edge. SUBJECT
```
