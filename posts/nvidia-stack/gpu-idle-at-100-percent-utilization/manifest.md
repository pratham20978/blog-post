# Asset manifest — gpu-idle-at-100-percent-utilization

Figures 1–10 are generated by `assets/_figs.py`, the thing to edit for a correction: change the data
or the layout in the function, re-run `python3 _figs.py <n>`, and the `.html`, `.svg` and 2× `.png`
are re-emitted together. The cover is rebuilt with `python3 _cover.py`.

Figures 2, 3, 4, 9 and 10 draw the worked example. They import `lab/queue_model.py` and read every
number from it, so a figure cannot disagree with the prose or with the lab: change an input in the
model and the figures change with it. Their captions say "model inputs, not a measurement".

## Route gate

No figure is graph-shaped enough to need Mermaid's layout engine: eight are timelines, grids or
small multiples whose value is a to-scale position, which the gate stops at Q4. Figure 5 has nine
nodes on three lanes and no crossings (Q5). Figure 8 is the one chart; it plots NVIDIA's measured
V100 numbers from the ledger. Figure 4 is a curve computed from the model, so it takes the `plot`
route; `_figs.py` hand-authors its SVG and `fig-04-queue-depth.svg` is its kept source alongside
the `.html`.

No paper figure is reused. The one candidate — Figure 1 of the PyTorch paper, a CPU/GPU trace of a
ResNet-50 step — is under arXiv's non-exclusive distribution licence, which grants no reuse right,
and Figure 2 here carries the same point with numbers the reader can check. The Source credit and
Rights columns are therefore `—` throughout.
