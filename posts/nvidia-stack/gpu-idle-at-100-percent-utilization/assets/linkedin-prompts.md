# LinkedIn image prompts — gpu-idle-at-100-percent-utilization

Six alternative image prompts, one per shared feed card, for the abstract editorial-illustration
route. These are not descriptions of the rendered cards in `assets/social/`; those are the shipped
assets and carry their own text. These prompts produce wordless illustrations in one visual world,
for use when a carousel needs imagery rather than typography.

The recurring objects are the article's own analogy made physical: a grid of burners (the SMs), a
long ticket rail with clipped tickets (the launch queue), two clock faces (the host and the GPU), and
a dial gauge (the utilization number). Keeping them constant is what makes the six read as one
sequence.

## 01 — One burner, a full gauge

- Narrative beat: The utilization number reads full while almost the whole chip sits idle.
- File: linkedin-01-one-burner.png
- Alt text: A dial gauge with its needle pinned at maximum above a large grid of cold burners, only one of them lit.

### Prompt

```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE
A 4:5 LinkedIn carousel opening frame showing a measuring instrument that reads full over a machine that is almost entirely idle.

SUBJECT
A round dial gauge with its needle pressed hard against the maximum stop hovers above a wide, shallow cooktop laid out as a regular grid of about forty identical cold burners seen from a gentle three-quarter angle. Exactly one burner, in the top-left corner of the grid, carries a small steady flame.

KEY DETAILS
Establish the recurring objects for the whole sequence: the grid of burners, the dial gauge, a long horizontal ticket rail with small paper tickets clipped to it, and two plain clock faces. The ticket rail runs empty along the back edge in this frame. Put the single atomic-tangerine accent on the one flame. The gauge sits in the upper third, the cooktop fills the middle, and the lower quarter is open white-smoke.

CONSTRAINTS
4:5 portrait frame, designed for a LinkedIn carousel. Editorial technical
illustration with flat vector-like shapes, fine consistent line work, subtle
paper grain, and generous negative space. Palette strictly limited to
jet-black #2d3142, blue-slate #4f5d75, silver #bfc0c0, white-smoke #f5f5f5,
and atomic-tangerine #eb6c36. White-smoke dominates; atomic-tangerine appears
once on the focal element and covers less than five percent of the frame.
Maintain the same visual world, scale language, line weight, palette, and
recurring objects across every frame in the sequence. One idea per frame.
No text, letters, numbers, labels, captions, logos, watermarks, signatures, UI
chrome, photorealism, stock-photo people, glossy 3D, lens flare, or drop
shadows. Keep meaningful content inside a five percent safe margin and use
large shapes that remain legible at mobile-feed size.
```
