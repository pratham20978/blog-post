# LinkedIn image prompts — laya-decision-model-explained

An alternative to the rendered social cards in `assets/social/`: five self-contained prompts
for an illustrated carousel. Every prompt repeats the locked style block in full.

## 01 — A ballot, not an essay

- Narrative beat: a decision model answers by weighing options it was given, not by writing
- File: linkedin-01-laya-decision-model-explained.png
- Alt text: A row of flat tiles with four hollow slots, each slot hanging a thread to a small pan, one pan much heavier

### Prompt

```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE
The first frame of a carousel about how a decision model answers a question. It introduces the recurring objects: a row of tiles and four hanging pans.

SUBJECT
A long horizontal row of flat rectangular tiles, like type in a composing stick. Four tiles in the row are hollow slots. From each slot a fine thread hangs straight down to a small flat weighing pan. One pan hangs far lower than the other three.

KEY DETAILS
The tile row runs across the upper third of the frame. The four pans hang in a line beneath it, evenly spaced, the heavy pan second from the left. The heavy pan and its thread are the focal element, in tangerine. Lower third near-empty. Flat editorial technical illustration, fine line weight, single point of emphasis.

CONSTRAINTS
4:5 portrait frame, designed for a LinkedIn carousel. Editorial technical
illustration with flat vector-like shapes, fine consistent line work, subtle
paper grain, and generous negative space. Palette strictly limited to
jet-black #2d3142, blue-slate #4f5d75, silver #bfc0c0, white-smoke #f5f5f5,
and atomic-tangerine #eb6c36. White-smoke dominates; atomic-tangerine appears
once on the focal element and covers less than five percent of the frame.
Maintain the same visual world, scale language, line weight, palette, and
recurring objects across every frame in the sequence. One idea per frame.
No text, letters, numbers, labels, captions, logos, watermarks, signatures, UI
chrome, photorealism, stock-photo people, glossy 3D, lens flare, or drop
shadows. Keep meaningful content inside a five percent safe margin and use
large shapes that remain legible at mobile-feed size.
```
