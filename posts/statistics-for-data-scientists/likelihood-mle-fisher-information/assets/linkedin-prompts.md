# LinkedIn image prompts — likelihood-mle-fisher-information

Five frames, one teaching arc: data raise one hill whose summit is the estimate, the MLE → three ways to measure how far a tested value sits from the summit, Wald, likelihood ratio and score → a lopsided hill where the three disagree, small counts → flooding the hill to a fixed depth, the profile-likelihood interval → a wall at zero that hides half the summits, the boundary.

**The finished social cards are already built** — see `assets/social/` and `social.md`, rendered in the house template with the post's own numbers. These prompts are the alternative route for a text-free illustration treatment from an image model.

Every prompt is self-contained. Paste any single one without carrying context.

## 05 — A wall at zero

- Narrative beat: testing Poisson against the negative binomial puts the null, no extra dispersion, at the edge of what the dispersion can be; in about half of all Poisson samples the summit would lie beyond that edge, so the highest reachable point is the edge itself and the likelihood ratio is exactly zero (52.6% of 200,000 data sets), which is why the right p-value is half the χ² one
- File: `linkedin-05-likelihood-mle-fisher-information.png`
- Alt text: Six stacked strips of hills beside one tangerine wall: three stand clear with flagged summits, three are cut off by the wall

### Prompt
