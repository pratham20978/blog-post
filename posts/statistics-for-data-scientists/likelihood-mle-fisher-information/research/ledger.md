## Open questions

- Wilks 1938, Chernoff 1954, Rao 1948 and Fisher 1925 were not read directly (Project Euclid's bot check,
  a Cambridge paywall). Their results are cited as stated by Brazzale & Mameli, Self & Liang, Lawless, Buse
  and Stigler, and the article says so where it attributes them.
- Efron & Hinkley, Molenberghs & Verbeke, Fears et al., Hauck & Donner, Dean & Lawless, Oehlert and
  Crainiceanu & Ruppert are cited for what their abstracts state, no further.
- Pawitan's *In All Likelihood*, a key source in the series plan, could not be read and is not cited; its
  role (likelihood intervals, observed against expected information) is covered by Lawless, Efron & Hinkley,
  Brazzale & Mameli and the lab.
- The one-parameter boundary mixture is asymptotic. At Clarke's n the lab measures 52.6% of samples on the
  boundary, not 50%; Lawless (L19) reports the same direction for the negative binomial. The article
  states the measured value.
