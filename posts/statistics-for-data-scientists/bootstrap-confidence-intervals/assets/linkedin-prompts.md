# LinkedIn image prompts — bootstrap-confidence-intervals

Five frames, one teaching arc: a sample is a handful from a bin → the bootstrap scoops from the handful again and again → it can never bring up a stone the handful lacks (the skewed mean) → its largest scoop is capped by the handful's largest stone (the maximum) → scooping single beads loses the string's order (dependence).

**The finished social cards are already built** — see `assets/social/` and `social.md`, rendered in the house template with the post's own numbers. These prompts are the alternative route for a text-free illustration treatment from an image model.

Every prompt is self-contained. Paste any single one without carrying context.

## 01 — The bin and the tray

- Narrative beat: a sample is a handful taken from a population; the bootstrap will treat that handful as if it were the whole bin
- File: `linkedin-01-bootstrap-confidence-intervals.png`

### Prompt

```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE
The opening frame of a five-frame sequence about the statistical bootstrap, told through stones: a large bin standing for the population, a small tray standing for one sample drawn from it. It sets up the two containers.

SUBJECT
A large open bin, its front cut away, full of rounded stones of many sizes, stands in the left half of the frame. To its right and slightly lower, a small shallow tray holds a handful of stones lifted from the bin. A thin curved arrow-like line of three or four stones in mid-air traces the path from bin to tray. The tray is the focal element and is outlined in atomic-tangerine.

KEY DETAILS
The bin, the tray, the stones and a small scoop are the recurring objects of the sequence. Bin and tray in fine jet-black line, stones as blue-slate outlines with pale silver fills of varied size. The upper third of the frame is empty white-smoke ground.
```
