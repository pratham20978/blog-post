# LinkedIn image prompts — hypothesis-testing-p-values-power

Five frames, one teaching arc: a design is a net with a fixed mesh over a lake of small effects → the net
holds only what is larger than its mesh, the exaggeration ratio → a catch on the wrong side, the type S
error → the same cast repeated gives different catches, the p-value as a random variable → an empty net is
not an empty lake, equivalence testing.

**The finished social cards are already built** — see `assets/social/` and `social.md`, rendered in the house
template with the post's own numbers. These prompts are the alternative route for a text-free illustration
treatment from an image model.

Every prompt is self-contained. Paste any single one without carrying context.

## 01 — The lake and the net

- Narrative beat: a design is a net with a fixed mesh: in the 2013 study's design only estimates beyond ±15.9 points reach significance, while the plausible true effect is 2 points, so the fish in the lake are far smaller than the mesh
- File: `linkedin-01-hypothesis-testing-p-values-power.png`
- Alt text: A calm lake of small blue-slate fish under an empty wide-mesh net with a tangerine rim

### Prompt

```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE
The opening frame of a five-frame sequence about statistical significance, told through a calm lake of fish and a fishing net with a fixed mesh. It sets up the lake, the fish and the net before anything is caught.

SUBJECT
A calm lake in cross-section fills the lower half of the frame, its surface one straight line, with many small fish of similar size at different depths. Above the water, a wide regular-mesh fishing net hangs empty from a straight pole. The net's rim is atomic-tangerine and the focal element.

KEY DETAILS
The lake, its small fish, the net and its pole are the recurring objects of the sequence. Water surface and net in fine jet-black line, the fish as simple blue-slate silhouettes, the lake's depth a pale silver tint. The mesh openings are visibly larger than every fish. The upper third of the frame is empty white-smoke ground.
```

## 05 — An empty net is not an empty lake

- Narrative beat: a non-significant result is not evidence of no effect: of 63 non-significant replications only 2 could rule out a small effect; showing absence needs a fine net cast on purpose, an equivalence test
- File: `linkedin-05-hypothesis-testing-p-values-power.png`
- Alt text: An empty wide-mesh net beside a fine-mesh net that holds only tiny fish, the fine net tangerine

### Prompt

```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE

SUBJECT
The same calm lake in cross-section with many small fish. Two nets hang above it side by side: on the left the familiar wide-mesh net, empty; on the right a net with a much finer mesh, holding many small fish like those in the lake and nothing large. The fine-mesh net is atomic-tangerine and the focal element.

KEY DETAILS
The lake, its small fish and the wide-mesh net are the recurring objects of the sequence; the fine-mesh net is new here. The fine net's catch matches the lake's fish in size, so it shows what the lake holds. Water surface and the wide net in fine jet-black line, fish in blue-slate.
```
