# Cover prompt — hypothesis-testing-p-values-power

Paste blocks 1–8 into an image tool. Blocks 4–8 are the locked house style and are
copied verbatim from `blog-visuals/references/cover-prompt.md` — do not edit them.

A deterministic fallback already exists at `assets/cover-hypothesis-testing-p-values-power.png`, built in
the diagram-design skin by `_cover.py` (title in Instrument Serif; Figure 1 reduced to its argument: the
distribution of replicated estimates from the 2013 design if the true effect is the plausible 2 points, cut
by the significance bar at ±15.9, the right-sign tail as the one tangerine area and the wrong-sign tail in
slate). It is the cover `manifest.md` lists; use this prompt only if an illustrated cover is wanted instead,
and then update the manifest's COVER row to `generated`.

The concept is the article's intuition: significance as a net with a fixed mesh. Whatever lives in the lake,
everything the net holds is at least as large as the mesh, so the catch tells you about the mesh rather
than about the fish. When the fish are small, the net holds only the rare oversized one.

**Alt text for an illustrated cover:** A calm lake full of small fish, and above it a wide-mesh net that holds
a single large tangerine fish.

```
1. CONCEPT
A calm lake seen in cross-section, like a page from a naturalist's field
notebook, with many small fish of similar size swimming at different
depths. Above the water hangs a fishing net with a deliberately wide,
regular mesh, suspended from a straight pole. The net is almost empty: it
holds a single fish, noticeably larger than any fish in the lake. That one
caught fish is the focal element.

2. COMPOSITION
The lake occupies the lower half of the left two thirds of the frame, its
surface a single straight line. The net hangs above the lake's right part,
its pole entering from the upper edge, the caught fish resting in its lowest
point a little above the water. The right third holds only the lake's
surface line and empty white-smoke ground.

3. TECHNIQUE
Flat editorial technical illustration in the manner of a field notebook
diagram. Fine consistent line weight, flat fills with no gradients inside
shapes, subtle paper grain over the whole frame. The water's surface and
the net in jet-black line, the small fish as simple blue-slate silhouettes,
the pole in blue-slate, the lake's depth as a pale silver tint. Only the
caught fish is atomic-tangerine.

4. PALETTE
Palette, strictly limited to these five colours and their tints:
jet-black #2d3142, blue-slate #4f5d75, silver #bfc0c0,
white-smoke #f5f5f5, atomic-tangerine #eb6c36.
White-smoke dominates as the background. Jet-black and blue-slate carry
structure. Atomic-tangerine is used once, on the single focal element, and
covers less than five percent of the frame. No other hues anywhere.

5. LIGHT AND MOOD
Even, diffuse light with no dramatic shadows. Calm, precise, and technical
rather than energetic. The mood of a well-made reference book, not a
conference keynote slide.

6. FRAME
16:9 aspect ratio, 1200 by 630 pixels. Keep all meaningful content within a
5 percent margin from every edge. The composition must survive being cropped
to a square and to a 4:1 banner.

7. NEGATIVE
Do not include: any text, letters, numbers, words, labels, or captions.
No logos, watermarks, signatures, or UI chrome. No photorealism, no stock-photo
people, no 3D chrome or glossy renders, no lens flare, no drop shadows.
No colours outside the stated palette. No busy detail — large readable shapes
only, since this is viewed at thumbnail size.

8. HOUSE STYLE
House style: an editorial technical illustration for a computer-science
publication. Flat vector-like shapes with fine line work, generous negative
space, and a single point of emphasis. Restrained and geometric. It should
look hand-designed for this specific article rather than generic tech art,
and it should sit comfortably beside clean black-and-white technical diagrams
without competing with them. Composed to stay legible at 300 pixels wide.
```
