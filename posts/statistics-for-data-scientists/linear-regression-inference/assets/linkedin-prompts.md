# LinkedIn image prompts — linear-regression-inference

Five frames, one teaching arc: a point casts a shadow → the line from point to shadow is perpendicular → nearly parallel rods cast one shadow however they tilt → a measurement loses its finest marks → the ring for the mean is not the ring for the next point.

**The finished social cards are already built** — see `assets/social/` and `social.md`, rendered in the house template with the post's own numbers. These prompts are the alternative route for a text-free illustration treatment from an image model.

Every prompt is self-contained. Paste any single one without carrying context.

## 01 — A point and its shadow

- Narrative beat: a small sphere hangs above a flat tabletop and its shadow falls directly beneath it: the fitted value is the data's shadow on the space the predictors span
- File: `linkedin-01-linear-regression-inference.png`
- Alt text: A small sphere above a flat tabletop with its crisp shadow directly beneath it; the shadow is tangerine

### Prompt

```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE
The opening frame of a five-frame sequence about linear regression, told through a sphere hanging over a flat tabletop and the shadow it casts. It sets up the idea that a fitted value is the shadow of the data on a flat surface.

SUBJECT
A broad flat tabletop seen from an elevated three-quarter view fills the lower two thirds. A small sphere hangs in the air above it, left of centre. Directly beneath the sphere its shadow lies on the tabletop as a crisp ellipse, the focal element, in atomic-tangerine.

KEY DETAILS
The tabletop, the sphere and its shadow are the recurring objects of the sequence. The tabletop drawn as a flat parallelogram in silver with a thin blue-slate outline and a faint grid; the sphere as a flat jet-black disc with one crescent of shade. The upper third is empty white-smoke ground.

CONSTRAINTS
4:5 portrait frame, designed for a LinkedIn carousel. Editorial technical
illustration with flat vector-like shapes, fine consistent line work, subtle
paper grain, and generous negative space. Palette strictly limited to
jet-black #2d3142, blue-slate #4f5d75, silver #bfc0c0, white-smoke #f5f5f5,
and atomic-tangerine #eb6c36. White-smoke dominates; atomic-tangerine appears
once on the focal element and covers less than five percent of the frame.
Maintain the same visual world, scale language, line weight, palette, and
recurring objects across every frame in the sequence. One idea per frame.

chrome, photorealism, stock-photo people, glossy 3D, lens flare, or drop
```
