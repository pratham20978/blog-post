## Checks the gate cannot make, and their results

- Every PNG was opened and read after its last render. The first renders had defects in eleven of the fourteen figures,
  none caught by a gate: σ̂ printed as "σˆ" (Geist Mono has no combining hat; figures and cards now say "residual SD" or
  "s") in Figures 1, 6 and 12; slope labels whose plates cut gaps in patient lines (Figure 1); a label on its own arrow
  and a plane label crossed by the plane's edge (Figure 2); a label plate hiding Longley's normal-equations label and two
  plates cutting dashed lines (Figure 4); dead space under the table (Figure 6); two colliding labels and a label on the
  dashed contour (Figure 7); a point on the frame edge (Figure 8); arrowheads over the rings (Figure 9); bracket labels
  cutting a fitted line, hiding a tick and running into the side note, and a table header whose spaces SVG collapsed
  (Figure 11); a label running into the next panel and a legend missing the right panel's lines (Figure 12); a legend
  wider than the canvas and labels on gridlines (Figure 13); reference labels over bar values and a dotted line with a
  dashed swatch (Figure 14). Figure 5's first Mermaid layout crossed four edges, put a label on a box edge, showed TSS
  as 185,009,000 (the capture's six-digit rounding; the lab now stores RSS and TSS at full precision, 185,008,826) and
  fed F without its TSS − RSS edge; the final layout has no crossings. `_overlap.py` then reported 0 issues on all
  thirteen drawn figures; on the cover it reports only its known false positive, the two serif title lines, and on the
  Mermaid figure only edge labels measured at one origin, another known false positive.
- `lint_visuals.py`: 0 text overflows; its arrow reports are Figure 5's Mermaid groups and Figure 9's free arrows, which
  end on rings rather than rects; the PNGs show every arrow landing.
- Number formatting: Figure 11's 2 mg coefficient is exactly 1.035, so it rounds half up to 1.04 (Python's float
  formatting printed 1.03); AIC is quoted to one decimal because the six-digit capture stores 233.235 for 233.2349.
- Card headlines and decks are claims: card 3's "squares the damage to the digits" became "doubles the digits the solver
  loses", card 6's "Few years did" became "the data hold few", and card 1's undefined "s" became "residual SD".
- KaTeX: rendered with the site's own unified, remark-math and rehype-katex: 0 errors in 10 display equations.
  Equations 4 and 6 first measured 739 and 808 px against the 686-px column and were split over aligned lines; the
  widest is now 572 px plus its tag.
- Numbers: a recheck script recomputed 111 numbers in the prose from `captures.json` with half-up rounding and found
  every one as written. The lab matches statsmodels to 5.6 × 10⁻¹² on the 39 numbers it compares and R to 1.2 × 10⁻¹¹;
  exact rational arithmetic reproduces all 121 of NIST's certified values; the article's code block was run and prints
  Table 1.
- Alt text: each figure's alt text above is extracted from `blog.md`, so the two cannot drift; the cards' alt text and
  transcripts in `social.md` are generated from the strings `_social.py` draws.
