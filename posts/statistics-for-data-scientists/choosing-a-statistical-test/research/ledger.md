# Source ledger — choosing-a-statistical-test

Part 8 of *Statistics for Data Scientists*. Researched 2026-11-01, inline (no agents). Numbers the article calls
measured or computed come from `lab/choosing.py` and need no key; every published number, date, attribution, named
result and library convention carries one below. Read in full: Delacre, Lakens and Leys 2017 and its 2022
correction, Delacre et al. 2019 (all CC BY 4.0, on the journal's site), Rochon, Gondan and Kieser 2012 (Europe PMC),
Yates 1934 and Yates 1984 (Rothamsted Research's repository), Boschloo 1970 (the Mathematical Centre's report
version, CWI repository), Campbell 2007 (the author's accepted manuscript, from his site) and Student 1908 (the
University of York's transcription, as in Part 2). Zimmerman 2004, Lydersen et al. 2009, Hayes and Cai 2007, Box
1954, Brown and Forsythe 1974 and Lancaster 1961 are cited for what their abstracts state. Fisher's *The Design of
Experiments* was not read: its tea-tasting design is taken from Yates 1984, §8, which describes it. Rasch, Kubinger
and Moder 2011 was not reached (Springer served a bot check); it is cited only for the conclusion its title states
and that Rochon et al. and Delacre et al. report. Welch 1947, Welch 1951 and Satterthwaite 1946 were not read; the
formulas are taken from Delacre et al. and R's documentation, which attribute them.

## Open questions

- Rasch, Kubinger and Moder (2011) was not reached; the article states only the conclusion its title and two papers
  that read it report.
- What Student and Fisher misquoted about the Kalamazoo trial is known here only from Senn and Richardson's
  abstract; the article says only that it was a within-patient trial.
- The mid-p convention is stated, not settled: Campbell describes two two-sided versions; the lab uses Irwin's-rule
  mid-p with ties halved, and the article names that choice.
- SciPy 1.18.1's `barnard_exact` tie handling is a finding of the lab (71 of 481 tables in the malocclusion design,
  each tied exactly with another table's abs(Z)); no issue report was searched for.
