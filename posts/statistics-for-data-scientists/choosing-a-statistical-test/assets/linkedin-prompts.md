# LinkedIn image prompts — choosing-a-statistical-test

Five frames, one teaching arc: twenty measurements are ten pairs bound by a person → read as two groups, the pairs are cut → pooling two groups of different sizes drowns the small one's spread → a pre-test sieve lets the wrong groups through → when the design fixes both totals, the test is exact.

**The finished social cards are already built** — see `assets/social/` and `social.md`, rendered in the house template with the post's own numbers. These prompts are the alternative route for a text-free illustration treatment from an image model.

Every prompt is self-contained. Paste any single one without carrying context.

## 01 — The pairs and their threads

- Narrative beat: every patient took both drugs, so the twenty measurements are ten pairs, bound by the person they came from
- File: `linkedin-01-choosing-a-statistical-test.png`
- Alt text: Ten pairs of small glass beakers in two rows, each upper beaker tied to the one below it by a thin thread; one thread is tangerine

### Prompt

```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE
The opening frame of a five-frame sequence about choosing a statistical test, told through glass beakers and threads: two beakers joined by a thread are two measurements on one person. It sets up the pairs.

SUBJECT
Two horizontal rows of ten small, plain glass beakers fill the middle of the frame, an upper row and a lower row, each beaker partly filled to a different level. Each upper beaker is joined to the beaker directly below it by a thin vertical thread, so the twenty beakers read as ten pairs. One thread, near the centre, is the focal element and is drawn in atomic-tangerine.

KEY DETAILS
The beakers, the threads and a plain table edge are the recurring objects of the sequence. Fill levels vary a lot from pair to pair but little within a pair. Beakers in fine jet-black line with pale silver fills; threads in blue-slate. The lower third is empty white-smoke ground.

CONSTRAINTS
4:5 portrait frame, designed for a LinkedIn carousel. Editorial technical
illustration with flat vector-like shapes, fine consistent line work, subtle
paper grain, and generous negative space. Palette strictly limited to
jet-black #2d3142, blue-slate #4f5d75, silver #bfc0c0, white-smoke #f5f5f5,
and atomic-tangerine #eb6c36. White-smoke dominates; atomic-tangerine appears
once on the focal element and covers less than five percent of the frame.
Maintain the same visual world, scale language, line weight, palette, and
recurring objects across every frame in the sequence. One idea per frame.
No text, letters, numbers, labels, captions, logos, watermarks, signatures, UI
chrome, photorealism, stock-photo people, glossy 3D, lens flare, or drop
shadows. Keep meaningful content inside a five percent safe margin and use
large shapes that remain legible at mobile-feed size.
```

### Prompt

```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE
The third frame of a five-frame sequence about choosing a statistical test. Two groups of very different sizes are poured into one estimate of spread, and the small, varied group is drowned out.

SUBJECT

Both tilt towards a single measuring jug in the middle, into which their marbles stream and mix. The small jar's rim is the focal element, drawn in atomic-tangerine.

## 04 — The sieve with wide holes

- File: `linkedin-04-choosing-a-statistical-test.png`

### Prompt

```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE
The fourth frame of a five-frame sequence about choosing a statistical test. A preliminary test is drawn as a coarse sieve that is meant to sort groups but lets nearly everything through.

SUBJECT
A wide, flat sieve with large square holes hangs in the upper middle of the frame. Below it sit two shallow trays, left and right. Several small beakers drop through the sieve's wide holes into the left tray; only one or two are held back above the right tray. One beaker, halfway through a hole, is the focal element and is outlined in atomic-tangerine.

KEY DETAILS
The beakers are the same as in the first two frames, small and plain. The sieve's mesh is drawn in fine jet-black line, its holes clearly too wide for its job. The trays are silver outlines. White-smoke space surrounds the scene.
