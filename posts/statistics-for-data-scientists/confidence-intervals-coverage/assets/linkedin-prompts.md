# LinkedIn image prompts — confidence-intervals-coverage

Five frames, one teaching arc: a confidence procedure is a thrower with a 95% record → the interval you computed is one ring in the air, on or off the peg → a ring with no hole can never land (the Wald interval at zero errors) → two rings can overlap while the pegs are truly apart → on skewed data the smallest rings fall short.

**The finished social cards are already built** — see `assets/social/` and `social.md`, rendered in the house template with the post's own numbers. These prompts are the alternative route for a text-free illustration treatment from an image model.

Every prompt is self-contained. Paste any single one without carrying context.

SUBJECT

Nineteen identical rings have landed over the peg and stack neatly around its base; one identical ring lies flat on the board a short distance to the right of the peg.
