# Source ledger — confidence-intervals-coverage

Part 6 of *Statistics for Data Scientists*. Researched 2026-10-31, inline (no agents). Numbers the article calls
measured or computed come from `lab/intervals.py` and need no key; every published number, date, attribution and
named result carries one below. Several central papers are paywalled or bot-walled: those are cited for what their
abstracts state, or for what an open paper that discusses them reports, no further, and each row says so. Every
published number quoted in the article was recomputed by the lab before it was used (Agresti and Coull's Table 1,
all 16 of the trial's Clopper–Pearson rows, Greenland et al.'s overlap example, the overlap rule's error rate), and
the article says where a printed value does not reproduce to the digit.
