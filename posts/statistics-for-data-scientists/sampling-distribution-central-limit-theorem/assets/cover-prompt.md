# Cover prompt — sampling-distribution-central-limit-theorem

Paste blocks 1–8 into an image tool. Blocks 4–8 are the locked house style and are
copied verbatim from `blog-visuals/references/cover-prompt.md` — do not edit them.

A deterministic fallback already exists at `assets/cover-sampling-distribution-central-limit-theorem.png`,
built in the diagram-design skin by `_cover.py` (title in Instrument Serif; Figure 2's n = 30 panel
reduced to its argument: means of 30 customers' spend in standard errors as bars, the normal curve the
CLT promises dashed in slate, and the true mean as the one tangerine line, with 71.8% of the means to its
left). It is the cover `manifest.md` lists; use this prompt only if an illustrated cover is wanted
instead, and then update the manifest's COVER row to `generated`.

The concept is the article's own analogy (Intuition): 30 soundings of a lake where one trench holds much
of the water miss the trench, read shallow and agree with one another, so they look precise and are wrong.

**Alt text for an illustrated cover:** A lake in cross-section with a shallow bed and one deep trench
outlined in tangerine; thin sounding lines all stop on the shallow bed.

```
1. CONCEPT
A lake seen in cross-section, its bed long and shallow except for one
narrow, deep trench that holds much of the water. A surveyor has taken
thirty soundings from the surface: thin lines with small weights hang down
from floats on the water, and every one of them stops on the shallow bed,
so none has found the trench. The soundings agree with one another and are
all wrong about the lake's average depth. The trench that no sounding
reached is the focal element.

2. COMPOSITION
The waterline runs straight across the image at about one third of its
height. The shallow bed lies a little below the middle, rising and falling
gently. The trench cuts down from the bed slightly right of centre, narrow
and steep, to just above the lower margin, so its mouth stays inside a 4:1
banner crop. The sounding lines hang at uneven spacing across the left half
and up to the trench's near edge, each ending on the bed. The right third
holds only open water above a flat, plain bed, and the band above the
waterline is empty white-smoke ground.

3. TECHNIQUE
Flat editorial technical illustration in the manner of a hydrographic
survey drawing. Fine consistent line weight, flat fills with no gradients
inside shapes, subtle paper grain over the whole frame. Water as a pale flat
tint of blue-slate, the bed in silver with fine engraved hatching, the
sounding lines and their weights in jet-black, the floats as small silver
circles. Only the trench's outline is atomic-tangerine.

4. PALETTE
Palette, strictly limited to these five colours and their tints:
jet-black #2d3142, blue-slate #4f5d75, silver #bfc0c0,
white-smoke #f5f5f5, atomic-tangerine #eb6c36.
White-smoke dominates as the background. Jet-black and blue-slate carry
structure. Atomic-tangerine is used once, on the single focal element, and
covers less than five percent of the frame. No other hues anywhere.

5. LIGHT AND MOOD
Even, diffuse light with no dramatic shadows. Calm, precise, and technical
rather than energetic. The mood of a well-made reference book, not a
conference keynote slide.

6. FRAME
16:9 aspect ratio, 1200 by 630 pixels. Keep all meaningful content within a
5 percent margin from every edge. The composition must survive being cropped
to a square and to a 4:1 banner.

7. NEGATIVE
Do not include: any text, letters, numbers, words, labels, or captions.
No logos, watermarks, signatures, or UI chrome. No photorealism, no stock-photo
people, no 3D chrome or glossy renders, no lens flare, no drop shadows.
No colours outside the stated palette. No busy detail — large readable shapes
only, since this is viewed at thumbnail size.

8. HOUSE STYLE
House style: an editorial technical illustration for a computer-science
publication. Flat vector-like shapes with fine line work, generous negative
space, and a single point of emphasis. Restrained and geometric. It should
look hand-designed for this specific article rather than generic tech art,
and it should sit comfortably beside clean black-and-white technical diagrams
without competing with them. Composed to stay legible at 300 pixels wide.
```
