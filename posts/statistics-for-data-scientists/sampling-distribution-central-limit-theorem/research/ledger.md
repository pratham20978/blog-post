## Open questions

- Berry (1941) and Cushny and Peebles (1905) were not read (AMS 403; PMC browser check). Berry's result is
  stated as Shevtsova's and the 2018 paper's introductions state it; nothing in the post depends on Berry's own
  constant. Cushny and Peebles' table is taken as Student printed it, which R's `sleep` matches.
- What Student and Fisher misquoted is known here only from Senn and Richardson's abstract; the post says
  "misquoted parts of the trial" and nothing more specific.
- Hesterberg's n ≥ 5000 statement is read in the arXiv long version; the published TAS article's text was
  not reachable, so the post cites the preprint and labels it.
- Wilcox and Rousselet do not state their lognormal's parameters; the lab's standard lognormal reproduces both
  versions' numbers within their unstated Monte Carlo error, which is the evidence for it. Their 2023 replication
  count is ambiguous (20,000 in the text, 50,000 in a caption), so the post compares their n = 30 value only
  against our measurement with its MCSE and draws no conclusion from the n = 100 value.
- Esseen (1942) carries no DOI and was not read; it is named with Berry through S3, not listed as a source.
