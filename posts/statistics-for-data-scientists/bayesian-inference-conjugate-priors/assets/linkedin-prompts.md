# LinkedIn image prompts — bayesian-inference-conjugate-priors

Five frames, one teaching arc: belief is spread before the data → many observations outweigh it → a few do not → flat on one scale is a hill on another → a sharp value weighed against a spread.

**The finished social cards are already built** — see `assets/social/` and `social.md`, rendered in the house template with the post's own numbers. These prompts are the alternative route for a text-free illustration treatment from an image model.

Every prompt is self-contained. Paste any single one without carrying context.

## 01 — The prior

- Narrative beat: before any data, belief is spread as small equal weights along a long balance beam, one weight for every possible value
- File: `linkedin-01-bayesian-inference-conjugate-priors.png`
- Alt text: A long balance beam on a slim pivot with small equal cube weights spread along it; one cube is tangerine

### Prompt

```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE
The opening frame of a five-frame sequence about Bayesian inference, told through a balance beam, small weights and marbles. It sets up the prior: before any data, belief is spread along every possible value.

SUBJECT
A long, thin horizontal balance beam rests on a slim triangular pivot across the lower middle of the frame. Along its whole length sits a row of small identical cube weights, evenly spaced, so the beam stays level. One cube near the left end, the focal element, is atomic-tangerine.

KEY DETAILS
The beam, the pivot and the small cube weights are the recurring objects of the sequence. Cubes in fine jet-black line with pale silver fills; beam and pivot in blue-slate. A plain table edge runs along the bottom. The upper third is empty white-smoke ground.

CONSTRAINTS
4:5 portrait frame, designed for a LinkedIn carousel. Editorial technical
illustration with flat vector-like shapes, fine consistent line work, subtle
paper grain, and generous negative space. Palette strictly limited to
jet-black #2d3142, blue-slate #4f5d75, silver #bfc0c0, white-smoke #f5f5f5,
and atomic-tangerine #eb6c36. White-smoke dominates; atomic-tangerine appears
once on the focal element and covers less than five percent of the frame.

recurring objects across every frame in the sequence. One idea per frame.
```
