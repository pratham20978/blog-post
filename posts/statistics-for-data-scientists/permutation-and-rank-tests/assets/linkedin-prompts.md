# LinkedIn image prompts — permutation-and-rank-tests

Five frames, one teaching arc: fifteen pairs grew together → a coin could have swapped any pair, and every pattern was equally likely → relabelling pours two groups together and dilutes a small group's spread → ranks turn sizes into tickets → two groups with the same middle can still differ in which tends to be larger.

**The finished social cards are already built** — see `assets/social/` and `social.md`, rendered in the house template with the post's own numbers. These prompts are the alternative route for a text-free illustration treatment from an image model.

Every prompt is self-contained. Paste any single one without carrying context.

## 01 — The pairs

- Narrative beat: fifteen pairs of seedlings grew together, one crossed and one self-fertilised in each pair, so the test compares within pairs
- File: `linkedin-01-permutation-and-rank-tests.png`
- Alt text: Fifteen pairs of young maize seedlings in four plain pots, each pair split by a thin divider; one divider is tangerine

### Prompt

```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE
The opening frame of a five-frame sequence about permutation and rank tests, told through seedlings, coins and tickets. It sets up the pairs: two seedlings on either side of a divider grew under the same conditions.

SUBJECT
Four plain round pots stand in a row on a table edge. Across them, fifteen pairs of young maize seedlings grow, each pair split by a thin vertical divider: one seedling on each side. Heights vary a lot from pair to pair but little within a pair. One divider, the focal element, is atomic-tangerine.

KEY DETAILS
The pots, the seedlings, the dividers and a plain table edge are the recurring objects of the sequence. Seedlings in fine jet-black line with pale silver fills; dividers and pots in blue-slate. The upper third is empty white-smoke ground.

CONSTRAINTS
```
