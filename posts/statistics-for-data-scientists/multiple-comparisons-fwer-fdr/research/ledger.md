## Open questions

- Dataset decision (not a claim in the post): both candidates are redistributable — qvalue's `hedenfalk` is LGPL (S19) and sda 1.3.9's `singh2002` (102 arrays × 6,033 genes) is GPL ≥ 3 (S21). Hedenfalk was chosen because the package ships the p-values with the 317,000 permutation statistics behind them and the paper published counts to reproduce; the prostate data would need Efron's z-values re-derived from a matrix whose normalisation may differ.

- The paper's BH counts (21, 88, 153) do not reproduce from the package's p-values, which give 24, 94 and 157 (24, 93, 153 with m = 3,226; 21, 94, 153 run as step-down; pFDR variants do worse). The package's p-values are close to the paper's but not identical (PDCD5: 4.86 × 10⁻⁴ here, 4.79 × 10⁻⁴ in the paper), so the q-value counts can be compared (80, 159, 231 against 80, 160, 231) but the BH gap stays unexplained. The post reports it as an open discrepancy.
- R is not available in this workspace, so `p.adjust` and `qvalue` were not run. Our π0 reproduces values R users report for these p-values to about 5 × 10⁻⁶ (0.6699224 against 0.669926 for qvalue 2.x; 0.6635132 against 0.6635185 for qvalue 1.x), but those reports are not citable. The owner's R 4.5.2 check (author's private check per `context.md`) is pending and goes into the captures.
- Efron's *Large-Scale Inference* (2010), listed in the plan, was not consulted: its host is unreachable here and the post does not rely on it. Efron 2007/2010 on correlated z-values were found but not read (browser check).
- Freedman 1983's full text is not readable here (Taylor & Francis refuses scripts); the post cites its abstract for the claim, Ding for the n = 100, p = 50 setting, and our own simulation for every number.
- Simmons et al. do not say whether their interaction model used dummy or effect coding, or Student or Welch tests. Our dummy-coded reproduction lands within about one point of every row; effect coding moves the four-way rate down by about three points. The post reports this as a fork of its own.
