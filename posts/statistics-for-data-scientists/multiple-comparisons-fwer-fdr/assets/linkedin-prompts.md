# LinkedIn image prompts — multiple-comparisons-fwer-fdr

An alternative five-frame carousel to generate with an image tool. Each prompt stands alone; together they follow one arc: the problem, the two promises, the mechanism, the consequence, the uncounted tests.

## 01 — Lucky noise
- Narrative beat: with thousands of tests at one threshold, some pure noise passes by chance and sits in the results beside the real finds
- File: linkedin-01-lucky-noise.png
- Alt text: A long row of sieves over one tray; most grey beads stay caught, a few slip into the tray beside dark beads, one falling bead highlighted
### Prompt
```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE
A 4:5 LinkedIn carousel frame, the first of five, showing why testing thousands of hypotheses at once lets noise into the results.

SUBJECT
A long row of identical round sieves recedes in gentle isometric perspective from the lower left toward the upper right, each catching a light shower of silver-grey beads falling from above. One shallow collection tray runs beneath the whole row. Almost every grey bead stays caught in its mesh, but a scattering have slipped through into the tray, where they lie mixed with a handful of solid jet-black beads. The recurring world of this sequence: round sieves with fine meshes, small silver-grey beads that stand for tests with nothing behind them, solid jet-black beads that stand for real effects, and shallow collection trays that hold whatever a sieve lets through.

KEY DETAILS
The single focal element is one grey bead caught mid-fall just below a mesh, outlined in atomic-tangerine: a false discovery as it happens. Fine one-pixel strokes for the meshes and rims, flat fills, and a calm empty band across the top third.
```
