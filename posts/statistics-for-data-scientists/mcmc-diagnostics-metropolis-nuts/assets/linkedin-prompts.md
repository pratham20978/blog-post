# LinkedIn image prompts — mcmc-diagnostics-metropolis-nuts

Five frames, one teaching arc: one step on a hill → a refused step still leaves a print → stride length decides coverage → walkers from far apart come to agree → agreeing walkers can all miss a second valley.

**The finished social cards are already built** — see `assets/social/` and `social.md`, rendered in the house template with the post's own numbers. These prompts are the alternative route for a text-free illustration treatment from an image model.

Every prompt is self-contained. Paste any single one without carrying context.

## 01 — One step

- Narrative beat: a walker at a point on a hillside looks at a spot a stride away and steps there if it is higher, or sometimes if it is lower
- File: `linkedin-01-mcmc-diagnostics-metropolis-nuts.png`
- Alt text: A small walker on a hillside with a dotted stride to a nearby spot uphill; one footprint at the new spot is tangerine

### Prompt

```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE
The opening frame of a five-frame sequence about Markov chain Monte Carlo, told through a walker who maps a hilly landscape by leaving footprints. It sets up a single step: look one stride away, and move if the ground there is higher, sometimes even if it is lower.

SUBJECT
A single smooth hill rises across the lower two thirds of the frame. A small, simple walker figure stands on its left slope. A dotted arc of one stride leads from the walker to a spot slightly higher up the slope, where one footprint, the focal element, is atomic-tangerine.

KEY DETAILS
The hill, the walker and the footprints are the recurring objects of the sequence. The hill drawn as a few flat contour bands in blue-slate and silver; the walker as a plain jet-black silhouette; footprints as tiny ovals. A faint regular grid recedes behind the hill. The upper third is empty white-smoke ground.

CONSTRAINTS

paper grain, and generous negative space. Palette strictly limited to
```
