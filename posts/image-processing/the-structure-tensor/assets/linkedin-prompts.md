# LinkedIn image prompts — the-structure-tensor

Five frames, one teaching arc: a detector's chosen spot beside the bright tile → each tile pointing
one way only → windows growing from a line to a lens → one sliver judged by two gates → a bead
sliding along a rail beside a bead pinned in a cup.

**The finished social cards are already built** — see `social/` and `social.md`, rendered in the
house template with the post's own numbers. These prompts are the alternative route for a
text-free illustration treatment from an image model.

Every prompt is self-contained. Paste any single one without carrying context.

---

## 01 — The chosen tile

- Narrative beat: the best feature to track is the window between the bright tile and the boundary, not the bright tile itself
- File: `linkedin-01-the-chosen-tile.png`
- Alt text: A tiled square split by a straight boundary, with one bright tile and a tangerine lens set beside it rather than on it

### Prompt

```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE
A 4:5 LinkedIn carousel frame, the opening image of a five-frame sequence about how a feature detector chooses points. It shows a detector choosing a spot that is not the obvious one.

SUBJECT
A square mosaic of tiles split down the middle by a straight vertical boundary, dark tiles on the left and pale tiles on the right. On the dark side, in the middle row, sits one bright tile. A single rounded lens shape rests on the dark tile between the bright tile and the boundary, touching neither, as if placed there by a careful hand.

KEY DETAILS
The mosaic is the recurring object of the whole sequence. Dark tiles are jet-black, pale tiles silver, the bright tile white-smoke, outlines fine and even. The lens beside the bright tile is the focal element and the only coloured shape. Flat fills, fine outlines, subtle paper grain, generous empty space above the mosaic.

CONSTRAINTS
4:5 portrait frame, designed for a LinkedIn carousel. Editorial technical
illustration with flat vector-like shapes, fine consistent line work, subtle
paper grain, and generous negative space. Palette strictly limited to
jet-black #2d3142, blue-slate #4f5d75, silver #bfc0c0, white-smoke #f5f5f5,
and atomic-tangerine #eb6c36. White-smoke dominates; atomic-tangerine appears
once on the focal element and covers less than five percent of the frame.
Maintain the same visual world, scale language, line weight, palette, and
recurring objects across every frame in the sequence. One idea per frame.
No text, letters, numbers, labels, captions, logos, watermarks, signatures, UI
chrome, photorealism, stock-photo people, glossy 3D, lens flare, or drop
shadows. Keep meaningful content inside a five percent safe margin and use
```
