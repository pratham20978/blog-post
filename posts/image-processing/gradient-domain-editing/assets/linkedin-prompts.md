# LinkedIn image prompts — gradient-domain-editing

Six frames, one teaching arc: a tray of twelve tiles around nine empty sockets, what is kept → a cross
of five tiles held level by strings, one equation per missing value → a sheet whose ripples fade but
whose broad dip stays, why sweeps are slow → a coarse lattice flattening the dip, multigrid → a sheet
tilting to meet a lower frame, a clone hiding its seam → a repeating strip with smooth joins, tiling.

**The finished social cards are already built** — see `social/` and `social.md`, rendered in the
house template with the post's own numbers. These prompts are the alternative route for a
text-free illustration treatment from an image model.

Every prompt is self-contained. Paste any single one without carrying context.

---

## 01 — The frame and the empty sockets

- Narrative beat: an image region is fixed by the values around its border and the differences inside it: throw the pixels away and keep only those
- File: `linkedin-01-the-frame-and-the-empty-sockets.png`
- Alt text: A square frame of twelve grey tiles around a three-by-three block of empty sockets, with nine small tangerine-edged cards stacked beside it

### Prompt

```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE
The opening frame of a six-frame sequence about rebuilding an image from its differences. It shows what is kept when the pixels are thrown away.

SUBJECT
A square tray seen from above at a slight angle: an outer ring of twelve square tiles in shades from jet-black to silver, and inside it a three-by-three block of nine empty square sockets. Beside the tray lies a neat stack of nine thin cards, each marked only by a short raised ridge, standing for the differences that were kept. The top card has an atomic-tangerine edge; it is the focal element.

KEY DETAILS
The tray of tiles and the stack of cards are the recurring objects of the sequence. The tray fills the centre of the frame; the cards sit to its lower right.

CONSTRAINTS
4:5 portrait frame, designed for a LinkedIn carousel. Editorial technical
illustration with flat vector-like shapes, fine consistent line work, subtle
paper grain, and generous negative space. Palette strictly limited to
jet-black #2d3142, blue-slate #4f5d75, silver #bfc0c0, white-smoke #f5f5f5,
and atomic-tangerine #eb6c36. White-smoke dominates; atomic-tangerine appears
once on the focal element and covers less than five percent of the frame.
Maintain the same visual world, scale language, line weight, palette, and
recurring objects across every frame in the sequence. One idea per frame.
No text, letters, numbers, labels, captions, logos, watermarks, signatures, UI
chrome, photorealism, stock-photo people, glossy 3D, lens flare, or drop
shadows. Keep meaningful content inside a five percent safe margin and use
large shapes that remain legible at mobile-feed size.
```
