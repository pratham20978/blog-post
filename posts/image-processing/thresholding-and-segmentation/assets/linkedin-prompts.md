# LinkedIn image prompts — thresholding-and-segmentation

Five frames, one teaching arc: a stake at the foot of a hill → nine stakes and two tidy piles → a stake
driven into the big hill beside a pebble → stakes that drop with the light → a basin filled from two
springs beside one broken into four pools.

**The finished social cards are already built** — see `social/` and `social.md`, rendered in the
house template with the post's own numbers. These prompts are the alternative route for a
text-free illustration treatment from an image model.

Every prompt is self-contained. Paste any single one without carrying context.

---

## 01 — Two hills one stake

- Narrative beat: Otsu always returns a cut, and on a clean histogram it places it at the foot of one hill rather than in the middle of the gap
- File: `linkedin-01-two-hills-one-stake.png`
- Alt text: Two sand hills with an empty valley between them, and a thin tangerine stake planted at the foot of the left hill

### Prompt

```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE
The opening frame of a five-frame sequence about choosing one number to split an image into two classes. It shows that a single cut always gets placed somewhere, whether or not the place is good.

SUBJECT
Two rounded hills of fine sand on a flat plain, a clear empty valley between them. A single thin upright stake stands at the very foot of the left hill, just where its slope meets the valley floor, not in the middle of the valley.

KEY DETAILS
The two hills are the recurring objects of the whole sequence. Hills in blue-slate with fine jet-black outlines, the plain in silver, the stake the only coloured shape. Generous empty sky above the hills.

CONSTRAINTS
4:5 portrait frame, designed for a LinkedIn carousel. Editorial technical
illustration with flat vector-like shapes, fine consistent line work, subtle
paper grain, and generous negative space. Palette strictly limited to
jet-black #2d3142, blue-slate #4f5d75, silver #bfc0c0, white-smoke #f5f5f5,
and atomic-tangerine #eb6c36. White-smoke dominates; atomic-tangerine appears
once on the focal element and covers less than five percent of the frame.
Maintain the same visual world, scale language, line weight, palette, and
recurring objects across every frame in the sequence. One idea per frame.
No text, letters, numbers, labels, captions, logos, watermarks, signatures, UI
chrome, photorealism, stock-photo people, glossy 3D, lens flare, or drop
shadows. Keep meaningful content inside a five percent safe margin and use
large shapes that remain legible at mobile-feed size.
```
