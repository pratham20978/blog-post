## Open questions

- OpenCV's docs say the Scharr aperture "may give more accurate results than the 3×3
  Sobel" but publish no figure for it. S22 gives the design objective, not a
  comparison on images. The post states the objective and does not claim a
  magnitude of improvement.
- Canny's ~20% figure (L29) is quoted widely; it is a cost against Canny's own
  analytic criteria, not an image-level accuracy measure. The post says so rather
  than repeating it as an accuracy number.
- Whether the 20% figure appears in the 1986 TPAMI paper itself or only in Canny's
  earlier MIT technical report could not be settled from the sources reachable here.
  The post attributes it to Canny's analysis and labels it as a criterion cost.
- S12 is a preprint and is labelled as one wherever used.
- No claim in this post rests on a measurement taken by us. Nothing was executed,
  so there are no timings, no benchmark numbers, and no measured curves.
