# LinkedIn image prompts — kernel-design-separability-borders-derivatives

Five frames, one teaching arc: the named derivative filters are one stencil with one setting → a
crude setting turns the gradient → a wider setting steadies it beside a bad pixel → cutting a
smoother short narrows it → at the border the filter invents pixels.

**The finished social cards are already built** — see `social/` and `social.md`, rendered in the
house template with the post's own numbers. These prompts are the alternative route for a
text-free illustration treatment from an image model.

Every prompt is self-contained. Paste any single one without carrying context.

---

## 01 — One stencil, many names

- Narrative beat: the named 3×3 derivative filters are one stencil with one free number
- File: `linkedin-01-one-stencil.png`
- Alt text: A stencil plate of three by three openings with shuttered outer rows and one handle, several faint copies behind it

### Prompt

```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE
A 4:5 LinkedIn carousel frame, the opening image of a five-frame sequence about how the numbers inside an image filter are chosen. It shows that several filters with different names are one shape with a single adjustable setting.

SUBJECT
A thin flat plate pierced by a three-by-three grid of square openings, like a printer's stencil. The middle column is closed. In the left and right columns the middle-row openings are fully open, and the top-row and bottom-row openings are partly covered by sliding shutters that all move together. A single shutter handle projects from the plate's right edge. Behind the plate, three or four faint outline copies of the same plate are offset slightly, each with its shutters set to a different opening.

KEY DETAILS
The front plate sits slightly left of centre at a gentle three-quarter angle and is the recurring object of the whole sequence. The shutter handle is the focal element and the only coloured shape. The faint copies behind are drawn in silver outline only. Isometric line work, flat fills, subtle paper grain, generous empty space in the upper right.

CONSTRAINTS
4:5 portrait frame, designed for a LinkedIn carousel. Editorial technical
illustration with flat vector-like shapes, fine consistent line work, subtle
paper grain, and generous negative space. Palette strictly limited to
jet-black #2d3142, blue-slate #4f5d75, silver #bfc0c0, white-smoke #f5f5f5,
and atomic-tangerine #eb6c36. White-smoke dominates; atomic-tangerine appears
once on the focal element and covers less than five percent of the frame.
Maintain the same visual world, scale language, line weight, palette, and
recurring objects across every frame in the sequence. One idea per frame.
No text, letters, numbers, labels, captions, logos, watermarks, signatures, UI
chrome, photorealism, stock-photo people, glossy 3D, lens flare, or drop
shadows. Keep meaningful content inside a five percent safe margin and use
large shapes that remain legible at mobile-feed size.
```
