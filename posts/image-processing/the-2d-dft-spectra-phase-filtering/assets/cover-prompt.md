**Alt text for an illustrated cover:** A fanned stack of transparent sheets, each printed with
stripes at its own spacing and angle, one sheet slid sideways out of line.

```
1. CONCEPT
A stack of thin transparent sheets, each printed with evenly spaced parallel
stripes. Every sheet has its own stripe spacing and its own angle, and seen
straight through, the stack adds up to a simple picture — an image built from
waves. The sheets are fanned slightly so each one is visible. One sheet has
been slid sideways out of line with the others: its stripes no longer meet the
rest where they did, though they are just as dark as before — the stripes say
how much, the slide says where. That slid sheet's edge is the focal element.

2. COMPOSITION
The fanned stack sits slightly left of centre, about one third across, seen
from a gentle three-quarter angle and occupying about half the frame's height.
The slid sheet projects to the right of the stack by a small, clear distance;
its exposed edge carries the only colour. Behind the stack, a faint regular
grid recedes. The right third of the frame is near-empty, holding only
white-smoke ground.

3. TECHNIQUE
Flat editorial technical illustration with isometric line work. Fine
consistent line weight, flat fills with no gradients inside shapes, subtle
paper grain over the whole frame. The sheets are drawn as pale silver planes
with jet-black and blue-slate stripes; overlapping sheets darken slightly where
they cross, with no transparency effects beyond that.

4. PALETTE
Palette, strictly limited to these five colours and their tints:
jet-black #2d3142, blue-slate #4f5d75, silver #bfc0c0,
white-smoke #f5f5f5, atomic-tangerine #eb6c36.
White-smoke dominates as the background. Jet-black and blue-slate carry
structure. Atomic-tangerine is used once, on the single focal element, and
covers less than five percent of the frame. No other hues anywhere.

5. LIGHT AND MOOD
Even, diffuse light with no dramatic shadows. Calm, precise, and technical
rather than energetic. The mood of a well-made reference book, not a
conference keynote slide.

6. FRAME
16:9 aspect ratio, 1200 by 630 pixels. Keep all meaningful content within a
5 percent margin from every edge. The composition must survive being cropped
to a square and to a 4:1 banner.

7. NEGATIVE
Do not include: any text, letters, numbers, words, labels, or captions.
No logos, watermarks, signatures, or UI chrome. No photorealism, no stock-photo
people, no 3D chrome or glossy renders, no lens flare, no drop shadows.
No colours outside the stated palette. No busy detail — large readable shapes
only, since this is viewed at thumbnail size.

8. HOUSE STYLE
House style: an editorial technical illustration for a computer-science
publication. Flat vector-like shapes with fine line work, generous negative
space, and a single point of emphasis. Restrained and geometric. It should
look hand-designed for this specific article rather than generic tech art,
and it should sit comfortably beside clean black-and-white technical diagrams
without competing with them. Composed to stay legible at 300 pixels wide.
```
