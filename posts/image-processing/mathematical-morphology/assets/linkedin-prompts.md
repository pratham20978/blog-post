# LinkedIn image prompts — mathematical-morphology

Five frames, one teaching arc: a stamp that fits a strip but not a lone tile → the same test run twice
finding nothing new → a stamp and its mirror twin → a landscape cut into slices, each trimmed by the
same stamp → sieves of growing mesh that sort the tile from the strip.

**The finished social cards are already built** — see `social/` and `social.md`, rendered in the
house template with the post's own numbers. These prompts are the alternative route for a
text-free illustration treatment from an image model.

Every prompt is self-contained. Paste any single one without carrying context.

---

## 01 — The stamp that fits

- Narrative beat: erosion keeps only the places where the probe fits inside the shape; a lone tile has no such place
- File: `linkedin-01-the-stamp-that-fits.png`
- Alt text: A cross-shaped stamp resting on a strip of pale tiles, with one loose tangerine tile beside the strip where the stamp cannot sit

### Prompt

```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE
The opening frame of a five-frame sequence about testing shapes with a small probe. It shows that a probe keeps only the places where it fits entirely inside a shape.

SUBJECT
A small square board of flat tiles. Two columns of pale tiles form a narrow strip on the right. A plus-shaped wooden stamp, five squares in a cross, rests squarely on the strip, fitting. On the left of the board one lone pale tile sits by itself; it is the focal element, too small for the stamp to rest on.

KEY DETAILS
The board, the strip of pale tiles and the plus-shaped stamp are the recurring objects of the whole sequence. Board and dark tiles in jet-black and blue-slate, pale tiles in white-smoke with silver outlines, the stamp in silver with jet-black outlines, the lone tile filled atomic-tangerine. The board sits slightly below centre with open space above.

CONSTRAINTS
4:5 portrait frame, designed for a LinkedIn carousel. Editorial technical
illustration with flat vector-like shapes, fine consistent line work, subtle
paper grain, and generous negative space. Palette strictly limited to
jet-black #2d3142, blue-slate #4f5d75, silver #bfc0c0, white-smoke #f5f5f5,
and atomic-tangerine #eb6c36. White-smoke dominates; atomic-tangerine appears
once on the focal element and covers less than five percent of the frame.
Maintain the same visual world, scale language, line weight, palette, and
recurring objects across every frame in the sequence. One idea per frame.
No text, letters, numbers, labels, captions, logos, watermarks, signatures, UI
chrome, photorealism, stock-photo people, glossy 3D, lens flare, or drop
shadows. Keep meaningful content inside a five percent safe margin and use
large shapes that remain legible at mobile-feed size.
```
