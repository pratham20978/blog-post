## Claims

|---|---|---|---|
| L01 | Diffusion models were introduced as a learned reversal of a gradual, fixed corruption process | S01 §1 | historical attribution |
| L02 | If the forward step is small enough, the reverse of a Gaussian diffusion is itself Gaussian — which is what makes the reverse step cheap to model at all | S01 §2.2, after Feller (1949) | asymptotic result in the small-β limit, for Gaussian and binomial kernels; not exact at large β |
| L03 | DDPM adopts that justification explicitly: its Gaussian reverse transitions are expressive only because both processes share a functional form when the β are small | S02 §2, between eqs (2) and (3) | DDPM's own setup, T = 1000 with small β; Ho et al. cite the result rather than proving it |
| L04 | The forward process has a closed form that jumps from the clean sample to any timestep in one operation: q(x_t\|x₀) = N(√ᾱ_t·x₀, (1−ᾱ_t)I) | S02 §2, eq. (4) | exact under the fixed Gaussian Markov forward chain with ᾱ_t = ∏(1−β_s); not true of learned or non-Gaussian forward processes |
| L05 | Every experiment in the DDPM paper uses T = 1000 steps with forward variances fixed to a linear ramp from 1e-4 to 0.02 | S02 §4 | the paper's own runs (CIFAR-10, LSUN, CelebA-HQ), on data scaled to [−1, 1]; the β are constants, never learned |

## Open questions

- **DBLP was unreachable for this entire sweep** (Anubis anti-bot on every mirror and endpoint),
  and the Semantic Scholar Graph API rate-limited every request. Preprint-versus-venue status was
  therefore settled from arXiv metadata plus the publisher's own proceedings pages. This is
  adequate for S01–S14 and S16–S18, all of which have a proceedings record. For S15 (Luo) the
  evidence is a single arXiv version with no journal-ref, which is strong but not conclusive;
  the post labels it a preprint on that basis.
- The ACM Computing Surveys **article number** for S13 could not be settled. Crossref records
  volume 56, issue 4, pages 1–39 and carries no article-number field; a secondary listing gives
  Article 105. The ledger and the post cite volume, issue and DOI and omit the article number.
- The exact CelebA variant and resolution behind S09's Figure 2 was not confirmed, so the post
  says "CelebA" without a resolution.
- Citation counts for the two surveys were retrieved from Google Scholar but are a popularity
  signal only; they appear nowhere in the post and support no claim.
