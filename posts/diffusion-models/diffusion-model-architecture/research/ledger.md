## Open questions

- No single backbone is universally superior: published comparisons hold architecture, data, compute, objective and tokenizer choices imperfectly constant. The post presents selection criteria, not a universal winner.
- “Diffusion model” is used broadly in practice for iterative noise-to-data transport systems, but flow matching and rectified flow are not literally DDPM training objectives. The post marks the boundary.
- Benchmark rankings move quickly and are sensitive to preprocessing and feature implementations. The article uses only a few benchmark numbers to illustrate setup dependence rather than claim a current leaderboard.
