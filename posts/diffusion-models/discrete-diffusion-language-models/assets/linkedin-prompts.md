## 01 — Tiles turned face down

### Prompt

```text

SUBJECT
A straight row of twelve small square tiles in a shallow wooden tray; about half are face up showing plain grey faces, the rest are turned face down and carry a light diagonal hatching.

KEY DETAILS
One face-down tile in the middle of the row is outlined in atomic-tangerine as the focal element; the tray sits across the upper half of the frame; empty lower third.

CONSTRAINTS
```
