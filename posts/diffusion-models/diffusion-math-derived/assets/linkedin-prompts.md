# LinkedIn image prompts — diffusion-math-derived

The alternative prompt pack, for when the carousel is generated in an image tool instead of built
from the locked templates in `assets/social/`. Six frames, one teaching arc: the intractable sum,
the teacher and student, precisions adding, one error priced five ways, the dropped weights, and
three formulations as one object.

Each prompt is self-contained — paste frame 05 without reading frame 04. The constraints block is
repeated in full every time, because that is what keeps the sequence looking like one sequence.

Recurring objects across the frames, so the arc reads as continuous: **smooth single-line hills**
for distributions, **a square field of discrete cells** for images, and **a single tangerine
element** marking what each frame is about.

## 01 — The intractable sum

- Narrative beat: a likelihood would have to add up every path a noisy image could take back to a clean one, which is too many to add.
- File: linkedin-01-diffusion-math-derived.png
- Alt text: A crescent field at the bottom with a dense fan of faint paths rising from it to a grain field at the top, one path marked

### Prompt

```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE
A 4:5 LinkedIn carousel frame, the opening of a sequence about where the diffusion training loss comes from. It depicts the problem: too many possible paths to add up.

SUBJECT
A square field of small discrete cells at the bottom of the frame, holding a simple lighter crescent. At the top of the frame, an identical field holding only even grain. Between them rises a dense fan of very thin, faint, wandering lines, dozens of them, each a possible path from the grain field down to the crescent.

KEY DETAILS
The two fields are centred horizontally, the crescent field in the lower third and the grain field in the upper third. The fan of paths fills the middle. Exactly one path among them is the single focal point, drawn slightly heavier in tangerine. All other paths are fine silver lines. Flat fills, no gradients inside any cell.

CONSTRAINTS
4:5 portrait frame, designed for a LinkedIn carousel. Editorial technical
illustration with flat vector-like shapes, fine consistent line work, subtle
paper grain, and generous negative space. Palette strictly limited to
jet-black #2d3142, blue-slate #4f5d75, silver #bfc0c0, white-smoke #f5f5f5,
and atomic-tangerine #eb6c36. White-smoke dominates; atomic-tangerine appears
once on the focal element and covers less than five percent of the frame.
Maintain the same visual world, scale language, line weight, palette, and
recurring objects across every frame in the sequence. One idea per frame.
No text, letters, numbers, labels, captions, logos, watermarks, signatures, UI
chrome, photorealism, stock-photo people, glossy 3D, lens flare, or drop
shadows. Keep meaningful content inside a five percent safe margin and use
large shapes that remain legible at mobile-feed size.
```
