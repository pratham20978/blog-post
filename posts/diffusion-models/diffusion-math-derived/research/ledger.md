## Open questions

- Luo's derivation of the score interpretation (S03 eqs. 131–151) writes ∇log p(x_t) — the
  marginal — in steps where only the per-example score ∇log q(x_t | x0) = −ε/√(1−ᾱ_t) holds
  exactly. The two agree in expectation over x0 given x_t, which is exactly the denoising
  score matching argument (S12). The post states the careful version and says why.
