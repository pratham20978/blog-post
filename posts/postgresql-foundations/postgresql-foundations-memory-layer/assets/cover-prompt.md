**Alt text for the cover:** Three vertically stacked, receding shelves of identical square tiles illustrate a staged movement or dependency structure. The wide top shelf is full, the shorter middle shelf contains a few tiles, and the medium bottom shelf is partially filled. A single raised orange tile on the top shelf is connected by a dashed line to the middle shelf, emphasizing that the tile must pass through the intermediate stage before leaving. A faint receding grid and horizontal baseline provide technical structure, while the large empty right side creates visual balance.

```
1. CONCEPT
Three shelves of identical square tiles, stacked vertically and set back one
behind the other like drawers in a plan chest. The top shelf is wide and full;
the middle shelf is short, a single row of a few tiles; the bottom shelf is
medium and partly filled. One tile on the top shelf sits slightly raised,
the only coloured element, with a thin dashed thread running from it down to
the short middle shelf — as if it may not leave until it has passed there.
The raised tile is the focal element.

2. COMPOSITION
The three shelves occupy the left half of the frame, aligned on their left
edges at about one tenth across, receding gently toward the upper right. The
top shelf sits at one third height. The raised tile is a little left of the
top shelf's centre. Behind everything a faint regular grid recedes. The right
half of the frame is near-empty, holding only white-smoke ground and a soft
horizontal rule near the bottom.

3. TECHNIQUE
Flat editorial technical illustration. Fine consistent line weight, flat
fills with no internal gradients, subtle paper grain over the whole frame.
Tiles drawn as rounded squares with a single hairline outline; the shelves
implied by alignment rather than drawn as boxes.

4. PALETTE
Palette, strictly limited to these five colours and their tints:
jet-black #2d3142, blue-slate #4f5d75, silver #bfc0c0,
white-smoke #f5f5f5, atomic-tangerine #eb6c36.
White-smoke dominates as the background. Jet-black and blue-slate carry
structure. Atomic-tangerine is used once, on the single focal element, and
covers less than five percent of the frame. No other hues anywhere.

5. LIGHT AND MOOD
Even, diffuse light with no dramatic shadows. Calm, precise, and technical
rather than energetic. The mood of a well-made reference book, not a
conference keynote slide.

6. FRAME
16:9 aspect ratio, 1200 by 630 pixels. Keep all meaningful content within a
5 percent margin from every edge. The composition must survive being cropped
to a square and to a 4:1 banner.

7. NEGATIVE
Do not include: any text, letters, numbers, words, labels, or captions.
No logos, watermarks, signatures, or UI chrome. No photorealism, no stock-photo
people, no 3D chrome or glossy renders, no lens flare, no drop shadows.
No colours outside the stated palette. No busy detail — large readable shapes
only, since this is viewed at thumbnail size.

8. HOUSE STYLE
House style: an editorial technical illustration for a computer-science
publication. Flat vector-like shapes with fine line work, generous negative
space, and a single point of emphasis. Restrained and geometric. It should
look hand-designed for this specific article rather than generic tech art,
and it should sit comfortably beside clean black-and-white technical diagrams
without competing with them. Composed to stay legible at 300 pixels wide.
```
