# Cover prompt — postgresql-foundations-storage-layer

Paste blocks 1–8 into an image tool. Blocks 4–8 are the locked house style, copied verbatim from
`blog-visuals/references/cover-prompt.md`. Do not edit them: they are identical to parts 1 and 2,
and that is what makes the three covers read as one set.

A deterministic fallback already exists at `assets/cover.png`, built with the series toolchain.
It has the title in Instrument Serif at x=560, the same grid as parts 1 and 2, and a single wide
band of page tiles, one of them tangerine, over a line of dots for the log. Use it on any day when
the post matters more than the cover.

**Alt text for the cover:** A single wide band of small square tiles, one of them highlighted in tangerine, with a thin line of dots running beneath the band

---

```
1. CONCEPT
A single long horizontal band of identical small square tiles, like pages laid edge to edge.
Every tile is plain except one, which is the only coloured element: the page that changed.
Beneath the band runs a thin, evenly spaced line of small dots, like a ledger written in order,
and the few dots directly under the coloured tile are coloured too, joined to it by a short
dotted stroke. The coloured tile and its dots are the focal element.

2. COMPOSITION
The band of tiles runs across the left half of the frame at about the vertical centre, from the
left margin to just short of the middle. The coloured tile sits about two thirds of the way along
the band. The line of dots runs parallel below the band, the same length. The right half of the
frame is near-empty, holding only white-smoke, so a title overlay has room.

3. TECHNIQUE
Flat editorial technical illustration. Fine consistent line weight, flat fills with no internal
gradients, subtle paper grain over the whole frame. Each tile has a single short line near its
top edge, like a header strip; no other detail inside the tiles.

4. PALETTE
Palette, strictly limited to these five colours and their tints:
jet-black #2d3142, blue-slate #4f5d75, silver #bfc0c0,
white-smoke #f5f5f5, atomic-tangerine #eb6c36.
White-smoke dominates as the background. Jet-black and blue-slate carry
structure. Atomic-tangerine is used once, on the single focal element, and
covers less than five percent of the frame. No other hues anywhere.

5. LIGHT AND MOOD
Even, diffuse light with no dramatic shadows. Calm, precise, and technical
rather than energetic. The mood of a well-made reference book, not a
conference keynote slide.

6. FRAME
16:9 aspect ratio, 1200 by 630 pixels. Keep all meaningful content within a
5 percent margin from every edge. The composition must survive being cropped
to a square and to a 4:1 banner.

7. NEGATIVE
Do not include: any text, letters, numbers, words, labels, or captions.
No logos, watermarks, signatures, or UI chrome. No photorealism, no stock-photo
people, no 3D chrome or glossy renders, no lens flare, no drop shadows.
No colours outside the stated palette. No busy detail — large readable shapes
only, since this is viewed at thumbnail size.

8. HOUSE STYLE
House style: an editorial technical illustration for a computer-science
publication. Flat vector-like shapes with fine line work, generous negative
space, and a single point of emphasis. Restrained and geometric. It should
look hand-designed for this specific article rather than generic tech art,
and it should sit comfortably beside clean black-and-white technical diagrams
without competing with them. Composed to stay legible at 300 pixels wide.
```
