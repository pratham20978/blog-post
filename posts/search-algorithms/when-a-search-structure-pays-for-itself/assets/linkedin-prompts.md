# LinkedIn image prompts — when-a-search-structure-pays-for-itself

Five frames, one teaching arc: a bridge built once beside the long way round → the crossings that repay it → a packed shelf where one new book slides the rest along → pigeonholes that scatter what a shelf keeps in order → one pigeonhole crammed while the rest stay empty.

**The finished social cards are already built** — see `social/` and `social.md`, rendered in the house template with the post's own numbers. These prompts are the alternative route for a text-free illustration treatment from an image model.

Every prompt is self-contained. Paste any single one without carrying context.

---

## 01 — The bridge built once

- Narrative beat: Sorting is a fixed cost paid up front, like a bridge, and it pays back only after enough crossings: 10 lookups for 16 keys
- File: `linkedin-01-bridge-built-once.png`
- Alt text: A short stone bridge straight across a river beside a long winding footpath along its bank, with a pile of leftover stones by the bridge

### Prompt

```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE
The opening frame of a five-frame sequence about when it is worth building a search structure before searching. It shows the trade between walking the long way every time and building a shortcut once.

SUBJECT
A river crosses the frame. A long footpath winds along its near bank and loops far away to reach the other side. A short arched stone bridge crosses the river directly. A neat pile of leftover stone blocks sits by the bridge's near end. The bridge's keystone, at the top of the arch, is the focal element.

KEY DETAILS
The river, the footpath, the bridge and the pile of stones are the recurring objects of the whole sequence. Path and river in tints of silver and blue-slate, bridge stones in blue-slate with fine jet-black outlines, the keystone the only coloured shape. Generous empty sky above.

CONSTRAINTS
4:5 portrait frame, designed for a LinkedIn carousel. Editorial technical
illustration with flat vector-like shapes, fine consistent line work, subtle
paper grain, and generous negative space. Palette strictly limited to
jet-black #2d3142, blue-slate #4f5d75, silver #bfc0c0, white-smoke #f5f5f5,
and atomic-tangerine #eb6c36. White-smoke dominates; atomic-tangerine appears
once on the focal element and covers less than five percent of the frame.
Maintain the same visual world, scale language, line weight, palette, and
recurring objects across every frame in the sequence. One idea per frame.
No text, letters, numbers, labels, captions, logos, watermarks, signatures, UI
chrome, photorealism, stock-photo people, glossy 3D, lens flare, or drop
shadows. Keep meaningful content inside a five percent safe margin and use
large shapes that remain legible at mobile-feed size.
```
