- Narrative beat: Four familiar algorithms share one cycle and differ in the mechanism that removes untested candidates.
- File: `linkedin-01-one-loop-four-searches.png`
- Alt text: Four search paths converge on one loop of frontier, probe, elimination, and repeat, with elimination highlighted.

### Prompt

```text
SCENE AND INTENDED USE
A 4:5 portrait LinkedIn carousel opener showing four distinct paths converging into one repeating mechanical loop.

SUBJECT

KEY DETAILS
Use four recurring path motifs for the sequence: ordered tiles, an overlap ribbon, a directional compass, and a layered proximity web. Keep the loop central, the candidate tokens large, and the act of removing several untested tokens immediately legible without labels.
```
