# AI-search observation protocol

- Choose stable prompts that reflect real reader questions; do not tune them after seeing the answer.
- Test only a small declared engine/locale set and preserve screenshots or exported evidence where permitted.
- Record brand mention and page citation separately. A mention is not a referral.
- Store the cited URL exactly, then verify that it resolves to the canonical page.
- Compare repeated observations over time; a single generated answer is non-deterministic evidence.
- Use Search Console's own AI-feature reporting when available, but do not infer prompt-level citations from aggregate impressions.
- Keep editorial changes human-approved. Observation does not authorize automatic rewriting or publishing.
