# AI Discoverability

Treat AI visibility as an observation problem, not a guaranteed optimization channel. Build a small set of representative reader questions, record the engine, locale, time, whether Canery was mentioned, whether a Canery page was cited, and the exact citation URL.

Improve citation fitness with clear definitions, an early direct answer, concrete examples, explicit source attribution, consistent entity names, crawlable HTML, canonical URLs, and factual structured data. These are also normal reader and search-quality improvements.

Never fabricate engine results, automate against a product in violation of its terms, or claim that schema markup forces a citation. Read `references/ai-search.md` before proposing an evaluation.
